SecondAsk

A failed-payment recovery agent that prices every retry and reminder before it makes one.

BRDO · Open research · in progress since November 2026

Bondholder Rights & Disclosure Observatory

What does an Indian corporate bond promise its holders, and how much of what later happens to that promise can an outsider trace from public records?

RepositoryResearch designObservability report

A bond’s protections sit in its trust deed; what happens to them afterwards is scattered across trustees’ reports, issuers’ filings, exchange approvals and rating rationales. BRDO joins them up ISIN by ISIN, and every statement in the record says who made it and points to a hashed file, a page and the words relied on. Starting from the breach reports trustees must publish, the trace can often reach the clause in the trustee’s own words, and then breaks at the last step, for a reason of access rather than existence: no contract could be retrieved, because the exchanges and platforms that hold them refuse automated readers.

  • 93ISINs listed in trustees’ breach reports
  • 1,254Evidence anchors, each re-tested against its file
  • 18Discrepancies kept open
  • 0Contracts retrieved so far

Not investment advice, and no finding about anyone’s compliance: a recorded breach means a trustee reported one, and no report is not evidence that nothing happened.

Built withPython, JSON Schema, SQLite, pytest, uv

Quant and data

Four questions, answered with public data.

a

Macro Event Study

Code

How do CPI, payrolls, PMI and FOMC releases move markets?

Release data from FRED and prices from yfinance; reactions measured in equities, FX, rates and volatility at T+0, T+1, T+2 and one week, with significance tests across regimes and a seven-panel dashboard.

  • FOMC rate surprise: β = 0.303 on next-day equity variance (R² = 0.05). Small, but economically meaningful.
  • Long bonds buy the rumour and sell the news.
  • VIX compresses after positive surprises, most at one week.
b

LedgerLens

Code

Do company filings follow Benford’s Law?

Ten years of 10-K reports for 30 US companies in five sectors, scraped from SEC EDGAR, tested against the first-digit law, ranked by a suspicion score and written up as automated PDF audit reports.

  • Retail filings deviated most, likely because prices cluster at points such as $9.99.
c

SPLM India

Code

Can free satellite imagery anticipate revenue surprises for Indian retailers?

Sentinel-2 imagery at 10 m through Google Earth Engine, an NDBI index over the flagship stores of 12 listed retailers such as DMART, TRENT and JUBLFOOD, and a divergence engine that compares physical activity with analyst momentum. Validated point-in-time against look-ahead bias, with a test suite.

  • A research framework aimed at ACM ICAIF, not a live trading signal.
d

Oceanic Edge

Code

Does port congestion show up in ship-tracking data before it shows up in prices?

An AIS vessel pipeline that computes geofenced dwell-time congestion indices for six global ports, a backtested signal and an XGBoost model, plus YOLOv8 detection of ships and container stacks in satellite images.
